Robot Rules

1. Follow the exact action, not the student's intended outcome. 2. Do not silently add missing steps. 3. Ask for clarification when the target, direction or quantity is necessary but missing. 4. Never act out dangerous or unhygienic instructions.

Teaching prompt

“What did this teach us about decomposition, sequencing and precise algorithms?”

Before the lesson

Use a blunt spreading knife; check food allergies and school food-handling rules; clean the table and hands; use fresh ingredients. If the robot is instructed to do something unsafe, pause the demonstration.
